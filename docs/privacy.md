# Corgi Ads Agent: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Corgi Ads Agent ("the app") is a hackathon project that helps the Corgi Ads business
create Meta ads and monitor their performance. It is used only by the Corgi Ads team
on ad accounts and Pages owned by the Corgi Ads business portfolio.

## What data the app accesses

Through Meta's Marketing API, the app accesses data about the Corgi Ads business's own
advertising assets:

- Ad account, campaign, ad set and ad settings (names, budgets, status, targeting)
- Ad performance metrics (spend, impressions, reach, clicks, click-through rate, cost per click)
- Creative assets the team uploads (videos, images, ad copy)
- Basic information about the Corgi Ads Facebook Page

The app does not access personal profiles, friend lists, messages, or data about
people who see or interact with the ads beyond the aggregated metrics Meta reports.

## How the data is used

The data is used only to create the business's ads, report on their performance, and
recommend changes to spend, which a team member approves before they are applied.

## Sharing

The data is not sold, rented or shared with third parties. It is processed by the
services that run the app (the agent host and the AI model provider) only to perform
the actions above.

## Retention

Performance snapshots are kept for the duration of the project and deleted when the
project ends.

## Data deletion

To request deletion of any data the app holds, or to disconnect the app:

1. Remove the app's access in Meta Business Settings > Integrations, or in your
   Facebook settings under Business Integrations.
2. Message the [Corgi Ads Facebook Page](https://www.facebook.com/0000000000000000)
   asking for deletion. Stored data is deleted within 7 days of the request.
